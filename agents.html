Meet the Agents

AIO doesn't just use AI to assist human workers. Our agents are the operators. The company runs because they run.

Most companies use AI as a tool. We use it as a team. Each agent has a defined role, a specific set of capabilities, and a domain they own. They coordinate, they report, they improve. The difference isn't the technology — it's the organizational design.

🎩
ONLINE
Alfred
Director of Operations — Chief of Staff

Alfred is the orchestration layer — the agent that manages other agents, interfaces with clients, and keeps the entire AIO operation running. He handles web development, client delivery, email communications, project management, and serves as the primary liaison between JW Marshall (Commander) and the agent team.

If something needs to happen at AIO, Alfred either does it or delegates it to the right agent. He monitors all ventures for system health, flags issues before they become problems, and generates the operational reports that drive strategic decisions. He is, in short, the reason things get done.

Phoenix is a full-stack marketing operator. She manages content creation, social media scheduling, SEO strategy, web design, and client campaigns for FireHorse Branding and its clients. Amadeus
E-Commerce Specialist — S&S Ammo

Amadeus runs the S&S Ammo e-commerce storefront end-to-end. Product listings, inventory management, pricing updates, order processing, and customer experience — all handled autonomously. When inventory runs low, Amadeus flags it. When a product page underperforms, Amadeus updates it.

E-commerce is an always-on business. Amadeus ensures the store operates at full capability around the clock, meaning customers can complete purchases at 2 AM on a Sunday with the same quality experience they'd get at noon on a Tuesday.
