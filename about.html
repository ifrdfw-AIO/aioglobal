// The Man Behind AIO

Jeff "JW" Marshall is an entrepreneur, military veteran, father, and the founder and CEO of AIO Global Inc. — an artificial intelligence technology company headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas. With 22+ active ventures spanning legal tech, healthcare, recovery support, marketing automation, and financial services, AIO is one of the most ambitious AI company-building operations in the country.

But the story didn't start with AI. It started with rock bottom.

Over 22 years ago, JW was the guy everyone had given up on. Addiction had stripped away everything — jobs, relationships, dignity. Recovery changed everything. Through the 12-step community, JW rebuilt his life one day at a time. He remains deeply active in the recovery community today, serving at Step One NA — the oldest group in Tarrant County with 48 years of continuous meetings — because he never forgets where he came from.

JW channeled that work ethic into building IFR (Integrity Foundation Repair) into one of the leading foundation repair companies in the DFW metroplex. He learned what it means to build something real with your hands, to earn trust one job at a time, and to turn grit into growth.

Then he saw what AI could do — not for Silicon Valley, but for people like him. People who work with their hands. People who know what struggle looks like. That's when AIO was born.

"The 10% holding 90% of the wealth is the problem. AIO exists to close that gap by creating wealth vehicles for everyday hardworking people."

Today, AIO Global Inc. builds AI-powered products designed to be wealth vehicles — tools that create real value for real people. Recovery apps for people who can't make a meeting at 2am. Legal information for people who can't afford a $300 consultation. Medical tools for doctors drowning in paperwork. Tax strategy for working families. Every product solves a problem that the market has ignored.

JW is a father, a mentor, and a community leader who believes that the people who know real struggle are the ones who will work hardest to better society. His philosophy is simple:

"Build once, scale forever. Every product we create is a wealth vehicle for someone who's never had a seat at the table."
