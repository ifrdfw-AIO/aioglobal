GPS Property Tax

Professional property tax protest and consulting — powered by AI-enhanced appraisal analysis

Live AI-Enhanced
Visit Site →

Texas property taxes are a disaster for homeowners. Appraisal districts routinely over-value properties, and most homeowners either don't protest or don't know how. GPS Property Tax fights those unfair appraisals — and they win.

Our AI systems pull comparable sales data, analyze appraisal district methodology, and build evidence packages that make the case for lower valuations. Kim Jones and her team bring the human expertise; we provide the data horsepower to back it up.

The protest season is April-May every year in Texas. During that window, GPS handles dozens of protests simultaneously. AI automation makes that scale possible without proportional headcount growth.

What the AI Does

  • Pulls and analyzes comparable sales from MCAD, DCAD, TCAD, and other CADs
  • Identifies methodological errors in appraisal district valuations
  • Generates structured evidence packages for ARB hearings
  • Tracks protest deadlines and filing requirements by county
  • Content marketing: educates homeowners on protest rights and process
  • Lead generation targeting DFW homeowners during protest season

Thor Taxes

AI-powered tax strategy and filing assistance — bringing the hammer down on your tax bill

Live AI-Enhanced

Thor Taxes was built on a simple premise: most small business owners are overpaying their taxes because they don't have a CPA who proactively hunts for deductions. AI can change that.

Thor's AI systems analyze financial data to identify deduction opportunities, flag potential issues before they become audit risks, and guide users through tax strategies that are typically reserved for clients paying $10K+/year in accounting fees. The strategy layer is AI-driven; the filing is handled by licensed professionals.

The integration of AI and human expertise is deliberate. AI can process thousands of transactions and identify patterns a human might miss. Licensed professionals ensure accuracy and sign off on returns. Together, they deliver a product that neither could alone.

Hermes Medical

Drug Interaction Shield™ and AI medical scribe tools for healthcare providers

Live AI-Powered

Hermes Medical addresses two of the most significant friction points in modern healthcare: drug interaction checking and clinical documentation. Both problems are well-understood. Both are inadequately solved by existing tools.

Drug Interaction Shield™ is a real-time interaction checking tool that goes beyond the basic checks in most EHR systems. It identifies multi-drug interaction patterns, flags contraindications based on patient comorbidities, and provides clinicians with actionable guidance — not just warnings.

The AI medical scribe component captures physician-patient conversations and transforms them into structured clinical notes, dramatically reducing the documentation burden that's driving burnout across the medical profession. Physicians spend an estimated 2 hours per day on documentation — time that should be spent with patients.

Baldur Parole

AI-assisted parole packet preparation and legal document support

Live AI-Assisted

Parole boards make life-altering decisions in minutes. The quality of a parole packet — the documentation submitted on behalf of an inmate — can be the difference between release and continued incarceration. Most families navigating this process have no idea what a strong packet looks like.

Baldur Parole was built to fix that. Our AI systems help families and advocates compile compelling parole packets: employment support letters, housing plans, reentry programs, and personal statements that speak to the board's actual criteria.

This isn't legal advice and we're clear about that. It's a tool that helps people who can't afford attorneys navigate a system that was never designed to be navigated without one. It levels a playing field that's been tilted for decades.

What Baldur Builds

  • Structured parole packet templates tailored to state-specific boards
  • Employment support letter frameworks for employers to complete
  • Housing plan documentation and community support letters
  • Reentry program identification by release location
  • Personal statement guidance — what boards actually want to hear
  • Timeline tracking for hearing dates and submission deadlines
